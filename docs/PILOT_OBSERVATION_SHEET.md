## Go thresholds (from the Sprint 13 brief — evaluate after the session)

All of the following must be true to consider this pilot session a pass:

- [ ] Onboarding completed in under 10 minutes with at most 2 hints.
- [ ] First spray activity (after onboarding, i.e. not their very first attempt) completed in under 90 seconds.
- [ ] No P0 or P1 security or data-integrity issue observed.
- [ ] Inventory/stock calculation matched the farmer's own expectation.
- [ ] The farmer could explain the compliance record in a way that shows they understood what it is.
- [ ] The farmer identified at least one clear, specific weekly-use value (not just polite enthusiasm).
- [ ] The farmer is willing to continue testing for the 7–14 day window.

If any box is unchecked, note why in the write-up (`Sprint_13_First_Farmer_Pilot_Report.md`) and treat it as a real blocker, not a rounding error.
