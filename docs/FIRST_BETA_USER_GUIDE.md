# Welcome to FarmOS — Getting Started

Thank you for trying FarmOS. This guide will get you from nothing to your first recorded activity in a few minutes. Nothing here requires technical knowledge — if something doesn't match what you see, that's useful feedback, not something you're doing wrong.

## 1. Create your account

Go to the sign-in page and choose "Sign up." Enter your email and follow the steps. You'll receive a verification code by email — enter it to continue.

## 2. Create your farm

After signing in for the first time, you'll be guided through a short setup:

1. **Your farm** — name, location, and (if you have them) coordinates. No coordinates? Skip them — you can add them later, and the Weather page will tell you clearly when they're missing.
2. **Active season** — the current growing year is filled in for you; adjust the dates if needed.
3. **Your first field** — a name and its size in hectares.
4. **Crop** — what's growing in that field this season.

At this point your farm is set up. Adding a product and an operator is optional and can be done now or skipped for later.

## 3. Add a field

You already added one field during setup. To add more, go to **Fields** in the sidebar and click **+ Add field**. Give it a name, its size, and soil type.

## 4. Add inventory

Go to **Inventory** (or use the dashboard's suggested next step) and click to add a product. Choose the right category — Crop protection (herbicide/fungicide/insecticide) or Fertiliser — to see the fields relevant to that product. Basic details take under 30 seconds; everything past that is optional.

## 5. Record an activity

This is the core of FarmOS. Two ways to start:

- Click **+ Record activity** on the Activities page, or
- Click **Quick Log** from any page (bottom-right button, or the floating "+" on your phone).

Either way, you'll be asked **"What did you do?"** first — pick Spraying, Fertilising, Sowing, Tillage, Scouting, Harvesting, or Other. Only the fields that type actually needs will show up. Spraying is the most detailed, since it's the one with legal record-keeping requirements (see below); scouting is the fastest — often just the field, date, area, and what you observed.

If you're recording a spray and don't have a sprayer set up yet, you'll see a small **"+ Add a new sprayer"** option right there in the form — no need to go somewhere else first.

When you save, you'll see a confirmation showing what was recorded, the updated stock for any product used, and — for sprays — a note that a compliance record was created automatically.

## 6. What "Spray Conditions" / weather information means

When you record a spray, FarmOS shows the current temperature, wind speed and direction, and humidity for your farm's location, pulled from a public weather service (Open-Meteo). This is **informational**, not a certification or legal approval — it's there to help you judge whether conditions are reasonable, the same way you'd check a weather app yourself. If your farm has no coordinates set, this section will say so plainly rather than showing nothing or guessing.

## 7. What FarmOS does not guarantee

Please read this carefully — it matters for how you use the app:

- **FarmOS does not verify pesticide product registrations, certifications, or legal compliance on your behalf.** Registration numbers, certificate numbers, and similar fields are recorded exactly as you type them, for your own records — they are not checked against any official registry.
- **The weather shown is not a spray-safety certification.** It's current conditions from a public data source, for your own judgment.
- **FarmOS is beta software.** Features may change, and this guide describes what exists today, not a finished, guaranteed product.
- **You remain responsible for your own regulatory compliance.** FarmOS helps you keep organized records; it does not replace your own knowledge of local rules or your obligations as an operator.

## 8. How to report a problem

If something breaks, looks wrong, or confuses you, please use the **Beta Feedback Form** (`docs/BETA_FEEDBACK_FORM.md` — ask whoever gave you access for the actual link). Include:

- What you were trying to do
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- A screenshot, if you can

Even small confusions are useful — if something felt unclear to you, it will feel unclear to other farmers too.

## 9. How to delete your test data or account

To remove test activities, fields, or products you added while trying things out, use the "Remove"/"Archive" buttons on each item — these are safe and reversible where the app tells you so (for example, fields with recorded activities are archived, not permanently erased, to preserve compliance history).

To delete your account entirely, contact whoever gave you access to this beta — account deletion is not yet self-service.

## 10. Safety disclaimer for crop-protection decisions

**FarmOS is a record-keeping and organizational tool. It is not a substitute for your own training, judgment, or your legal obligations as a crop-protection product operator.** Always follow the product label, your national and EU regulations, and your own certification requirements when making spraying decisions. Nothing in this app should be treated as professional agronomic or legal advice.
