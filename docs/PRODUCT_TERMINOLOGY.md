# Product terminology

## Sprint 24 financial terms

- **Recorded costs:** field-season operational costs; missing sources are not zero.
- **Recorded revenue:** received field-season revenue; expected/approved is separate.
- **Gross margin:** recorded revenue minus recorded operational costs; not net/statutory profit.
- **Unallocated:** a real record not assigned to a field; FarmOS does not assign it silently.
- **Not recorded:** no trustworthy value exists; distinct from recorded zero.
- **Historical cost snapshot:** quantity and valuation frozen at activity completion.
- **Weighted-average cost:** unit cost after combining valued stock and a purchase.
- **Completeness:** whether evidence needed for a calculation exists.
- **Break-even:** shown only when completeness and units permit it.
